Netti · brand · set two

Premium directions

Draft · 29 Oct 2026 · Set one →

Seven new directions that try to feel like a fashion house rather than an app. They all stay in Unbounded, the Netti font, but each adds one drawn detail (a seam, a bias cut, an engraving, a crest) and moves to a quieter palette of noir, bone, blush and champagne, with Netti pink kept as the signature. Each is shown on a shopping bag, a hang tag, the app icon and the app header.
